## 2026-11-12 · First release
**Added:** Read public Facebook Pages, posts, comments, Marketplace listings and Ad Library ads through vendors, logged out, with a vendor fallback chain and spend limits.
**Added:** Connect your own Page once and read its Messenger inbox, posts and comments, leads, live videos and statistics. Read-only: the tool cannot post, reply or message.
**Added:** Webhook receiver commands and the data-deletion callback that Facebook requires.
**Added:** One row per fact, never twice. Running again adds nothing. Each cust sees only its own data.
**Added:** Consent, retention, export (`dump`) and full removal (`purge`).
