## First release
**Added:** Collect public Zalo pages politely, with a fallback helper only when a site says "forbidden", errors or times out.
**Added:** Connect your own Official Account and read followers, chats, articles and the message quota. Import Mini App numbers from your own export file.
**Added:** Rows are never stored twice, and one customer can never see another's data.
**Added:** Signed delivery to your web address, with retries and a parked list for failures.
**Added:** Export and full removal of a customer's data, plus a keep-time for private chat rows.
**Added:** An optional, read-only personal-account reader that cuts itself off at the first sign of trouble.
